ChatGPT said:

Good q 👌 Here’s the breakdown on intermittent fasting (IF) / 2 meals a day when you’re trying to get shredded:

✅ Why IF can help

  • Easier deficit: Skipping a meal = less chances to snack/junk.

  • Satiation: Bigger meals → feel fuller than 5 small ones.

  • Simplicity: Less cooking/cleaning, fits student life.

  • Insulin sensitivity: Some people feel more stable energy, fewer crashes.

⚠️ But watch out

  • Protein distribution: Muscle growth/retention is best with protein spread over 3–4+ feedings. With only 2 meals, you gotta pack ~70–80 g protein in each.

  • Workouts: If you train fasted, you might feel weak (especially heavy lifts).

  • Adherence: If you end up starving at night and binge, it kills the point.

🚀 Best setup if you want to try

  • 16:8 IF (fast ~16 hrs, eat ~8 hrs window).

  • Example:

    • Meal 1 (2–3 pm): Protein + carbs (fuel for gym).

    • Meal 2 (8–9 pm): Protein + fats + veggies + some carbs.

  • Add a protein shake or small snack around workout if needed (still keeps “2 big meals” vibe).

My take

  • IF isn’t magic. Fat loss = calorie deficit either way.

  • If 2 meals makes deficit easier for you → go for it.

  • If you feel weak or can’t hit protein, stick with 3–4 meals.

⚡Bottom line: 2 meals/day can work for fat loss, but for keeping muscle at 5–10% body fat, make sure protein is high and training stays strong.

  • Not letting it wreck your weight training recovery.

  • Staying in a deficit overall.
